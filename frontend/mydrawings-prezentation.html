A mobile app for construction drawings and wall type specs — on the Apple App Store and Google Play. One company account. Many sites. Each worker sees only the job they are on.

Drawings change. Prints get lost. The cabin is far from the floor. My Drawings puts the live pack in the pocket — structured by category, ready offline.

Open a drawing in two taps. Search by number. Filter by trade. When a manager adds or updates a PDF, the phone can notify the crew.

FAQ

Questions companies ask first.

What is My Drawings?

My Drawings is a mobile app from Proconix for construction drawings and wall type specifications. It is on the App Store and Google Play. Built for site companies: one firm, many sites, drawings on every worker’s phone.

Who is it created for?

For the people on the job. Dryliners, supervisors, and site managers who need the current drawing and the wall type spec without going back to the cabin. The head person and site manager run the pack. Workers just open it.

How does it help workers?

They search by number, filter by category, and open the PDF in two taps. Wall Types sit next to the drawing. If the signal drops, downloaded files still open. When a new revision is published, they can get a notification.

Is it on the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. My Drawings is a mobile app published on the Apple App Store and on Google Play. Same account on iPhone and Android. Push notifications work on both when a drawing is added or updated.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Workers download the drawings they need. After that the app works online and offline. Basements, cores, and sites with poor signal still have the pack.

Who uploads and updates the drawings?

The manager. The administration panel is where drawings are added, replaced, and sorted into categories. The same panel holds users, the site access code, and the SPEC pack.

Can one company have more than one site?

Yes. My Drawings is multi-site. Each site keeps its own drawings, wall types, workers, and site manager. A worker on site A does not see site B.

How are the drawings organised?

By category — Architectural, Structural, MEP, Electrical, or the categories the company sets. Search sits on top. The list stays short and readable on a phone.

Is it hard to use?

No. Download the app from the App Store or Google Play. Sign in with email and a short site code. No password to forget on site. Open, search, tap. Managers use a simple admin panel.
